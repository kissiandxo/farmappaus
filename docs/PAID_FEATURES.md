# Farm Direct paid features

## First candidate: Weekend Planner

Offer a one-time **A$4.99** unlock when someone wants to organise a second multi-stop outing. Treat the price as a launch hypothesis. Let everyone create one complete outing first. A planner would group saved stops, let shoppers reorder them, show published opening hours together and open each leg in Maps. The ordinary directory, meat and produce filters, opening hours, directions and favourites would stay free.

Use a StoreKit non-consumable for the unlock. Show Apple's localised price, support Restore Purchases, and keep trips usable offline. Repeated usage of the free outing is the signal to test before building paid features. A voluntary in-app prompt or opt-in tester feedback can supply that evidence without adding tracking to the release.

A purchase sheet could show the first stop names from the shopper’s second outing, followed by “Keep more weekend trips”, “Unlock once” with the local StoreKit price, “Keep planning for free” and “Restore purchases”. Dismissing or cancelling the sheet should preserve the draft and the first outing. People who previously purchased should regain access after reinstalling through their App Store account.

## Later candidate: Market reminders

Offer saved reminders for market mornings and recurring shopping trips. Device-scheduled reminders fit the one-time planner purchase. Consider a subscription only if Farm Direct acquires a regularly updated, verified feed of closures, changed hours or vendor appearances. The current bundled directory cannot promise live stock or change alerts.

Wanderlog offers paying members automatic stop ordering for a day’s itinerary. That gives us a specific feature to test if shoppers say choosing the order of their stops is the hard part. [Wanderlog route optimisation](https://help.wanderlog.com/hc/en-us/articles/13545624787867-Optimize-route)

AnyList's premium meal calendar and store-specific prices are a comparable paid planning use case. Farm Direct's proposed A$4.99 is an untested Australian price, independent of AnyList's pricing. [AnyList Complete](https://www.anylist.com/complete)

Farm Direct 1.0 is free. Both paid features are proposals for a later release. [Apple in-app purchase types](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/in-app-purchase-types/)
